What is this dashboard?

Sharp Signal monitors live World Cup betting odds. When a selection's odds move in a statistically unusual way, it raises a signal — a flag that something noteworthy just happened in the market. Signals resolve to correct or incorrect once the match finishes, feeding the accuracy score above.

Shortening
Odds decreased — the outcome is now considered more likely.
Drifting
Odds increased — the outcome is now considered less likely.
Confidence
How unusual the move was (0–100). Higher means a stronger, more significant signal.
