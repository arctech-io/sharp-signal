👋 What is this? Sharp Signal monitors World Cup betting odds in real time. When odds move in a statistically unusual way (e.g. a team's odds suddenly shorten by 10%), it creates a signal — an alert that something noteworthy happened. These may indicate smart money, insider knowledge, or changing conditions.

📖 Show glossary
Signal
An alert triggered when odds move in a statistically unusual way. Each signal tracks one market (e.g. "over 2.5 goals") for one match.
Shortening 🟢
Odds are getting shorter (smaller number) — the market thinks this outcome is becoming more likely.
Drifting 🔴
Odds are getting longer (bigger number) — the market thinks this outcome is becoming less likely.
Confidence
A score from 0–100 indicating how statistically unusual the odds movement was. Higher confidence = more likely to be a real signal (not random noise).
Z-score
A statistical measure of how many standard deviations the latest odds are from the recent average. Higher = more unusual.
Accuracy
After matches finish, signals are compared against the actual result. Accuracy = percentage of correct predictions.
